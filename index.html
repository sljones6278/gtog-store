What it looks at

  • EMA trend direction – the broader directional bias.
  • VWAP position – whether price is above or below the session's volume-weighted average.
  • RSI momentum – directional momentum and strength or weakness.
  • MACD confirmation – alignment between the MACD and signal line.
  • ATR volatility filter – separates active conditions from suppressed volatility.
  • Momentum confluence – multiple conditions must align before a directional signal appears.
  • Structured risk management – predefined stops, profit targets, breakeven logic, and a larger runner target.
  • Trade management – designed to take partial profits while the remaining position can participate in larger moves.
